KNOWLEDGE BASE ARTICLE #KB-042
──────────────────────────────────────────
Title: Account Password Reset Procedure
Last Updated: 2026-06-15
Product: All plans
Category: Account Management

──────────────────────────────────────────

Symptom: User cannot log in and/or did not receive password reset email.

Root Causes:
1. User may be typing incorrect password (check Caps Lock, num lock)
2. Password reset email may be in spam/promotions folder
3. Company email server may have delayed delivery (up to 15 min)
4. Account may be locked after 5+ failed attempts (auto-unlocks after 30 min)

Resolution Steps:

Step 1: Verify identity
   - Ask user to confirm their registered email address
   - Ask for their account/company name from the ticket

Step 2: Initiate password reset
   - Navigate to https://app.agenticbox.com/forgot-password
   - Enter the user's registered email
   - Click "Send Reset Link"

Step 3: Check email delivery
   - Ask user to check spam/promotions folder
   - Ask user to whitelist "noreply@agenticbox.com"
   - If using corporate email, ask IT to check email logs for delay

Step 4: Manual reset (if Steps 2-3 fail after 1 hour)
   - Escalate to Tier 2 with user's account ID and email
   - Tier 2 can trigger a manual password reset from Admin Dashboard

Step 5: Account unlock
   - If user was locked out after failed attempts, account auto-unlocks
   - User can try again after 30 minutes
   - For urgent unlocks, Tier 2 can force unlock from Admin Dashboard

Escalation Criteria:
- User has been waiting > 4 hours
- User cannot access their registered email
- Account appears compromised (suspicious login attempts from unknown IPs)
- VIP customer (Enterprise plan, named account)
